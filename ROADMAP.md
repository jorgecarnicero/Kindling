# Kindling roadmap

What's shipped and what's next. Ideas move up as they get picked.

## 1.0 – first release (Google Play, in testing)

180 topics in 9 categories (EN/ES), sparks and follow-ups, adjustable timer with a draining ring, day/night campfire themes with optional fire sound, favorites and sharing, no accounts or tracking.

## 1.1 – Your own topics (built, in testing)

Write your own questions (inside jokes, team questions, family classics), with an optional spark and follow-up, saved only on the phone. A tenth "Yours" tile (kindling-wood color) plays them alone or mixed with other categories, at any depth. Manage them from the pencil button on the welcome screen. They can be saved as favorites and shared like any topic.

**Fix:** some Spanish category names were cut on narrow phones (single long words). "Conocerse" is now "Romper el hielo" and "¿Qué prefieres?" is now "¿Esto o aquello?", and tiles shrink text slightly instead of cutting.

## 1.2 – Road trip (built, in testing)

A new way to play for long rides: pick one big theme (or let the app pick) and explore it along a road of 8 stops, each a little deeper than the last, and each with its own spark for a concrete way in. A passenger taps "Next stop" when the talk fades; there's no timer. A progress line shows how far you've travelled, and the end of the road offers another theme. An optional speaker button reads each stop aloud with the phone's own voice so the driver can join. The screen stays on and the fire sound rests. First themes, made for friends and full of stories: Friendship, Travel stories, Cringe moments, School days, Childhood, Food, Dream life.

## 1.3 – Characters (built, in testing)

Each phone's owner gets a character: a little person in the Matchbox sticker style, made the first time you open it (skin, hair, hair color, hoodie, or a random look) and shown as a portrait on the welcome screen. The backpack holds hats, glasses, hoodies, hair colors and things to hold. Some are free, others are earned by finishing Road trip themes and playing on different days. Finishing a trip that unlocks something shows "You found something!" with sparks from the fire, and your character rides along the trip's progress line.

**Fix:** "Another theme" at the end of a road trip no longer marks the next theme as done before it's played.

## 2.0 – Friends around the fire (planned)

Bring your friends to the fire. Share your friend card (QR, link or code) and add friends; their character and name stay on your phone. Before playing, "Who's here?" sits your friends and guests on the logs, and turn mode gives everyone a turn: the person whose turn it is sits in the glow and the card says "Ana, your turn". Each friend has a page with how long you've been friends and how many nights you've spent at the fire together. Still offline, no accounts, no data collected. Full plan: `docs/2.0-friends.md`.

## After 2.0 (planned)

- **2.1 – Your story together:** full stats with each friend (topics talked about, your top category as a title like "Deep talk buddies", time by the fire, trips finished together, your first topic together)
- **2.2 – Friendship items:** cosmetics earned by playing with friends (friendship bracelet, matching beanies, campfire mug, fire crown)
- **3.0 – Online (someday):** group mode with everyone seeing the same card on their own phone, accounts, friends and stats synced across phones

## Ideas for later

**Quick wins (no server)**
- Embers: an end-of-round summary (topics covered, the hottest topic, time talked), shareable as an image
- Who's here?: filter by pair, small group or party (topics are already tagged)
- Growing flame: the card's flame grows each time you add time

**Content**
- 300+ topics (about 35 per category)
- Themed packs: seasonal (Christmas, summer, New Year's Eve) and situations (road trip, first date, strangers)
- New categories: Nostalgia, Travel, Food, Kids ask
- More languages: Portuguese, French, Italian

**Bigger projects**
- AI topics for a described situation (needs a small backend; privacy policy changes)
- Daily topic notification or home-screen widget
- iPhone version on the App Store

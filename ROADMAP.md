# Kindling roadmap

What's shipped and what's next. Ideas move up as they get picked.

## 1.0 – first release (Google Play, in testing)

180 topics in 9 categories (EN/ES), sparks and follow-ups, adjustable timer with a draining ring, day/night campfire themes with optional fire sound, favorites and sharing, no accounts or tracking.

## 1.1 – Your own topics (built, in testing)

Write your own questions (inside jokes, team questions, family classics), with an optional spark and follow-up, saved only on the phone. A tenth "Yours" tile (kindling-wood color) plays them alone or mixed with other categories, at any depth. Manage them from the pencil button on the welcome screen. They can be saved as favorites and shared like any topic.

**Fix:** some Spanish category names were cut on narrow phones (single long words). "Conocerse" is now "Romper el hielo" and "¿Qué prefieres?" is now "¿Esto o aquello?", and tiles shrink text slightly instead of cutting.

## 1.2 – Road trip mode

Hands-free play: the app reads each topic aloud, with big text and auto-advance, so nobody has to look at the screen.

## Ideas for later

**Quick wins (no server)**
- Embers: an end-of-round summary (topics covered, the hottest topic, time talked), shareable as an image
- Turn mode: player names, each card says whose turn it is
- Who's here?: filter by pair, small group or party (topics are already tagged)
- Growing flame: the card's flame grows each time you add time

**Content**
- 300+ topics (about 35 per category)
- Themed packs: seasonal (Christmas, summer, New Year's Eve) and situations (road trip, first date, strangers)
- New categories: Nostalgia, Travel, Food, Kids ask
- More languages: Portuguese, French, Italian

**Bigger projects**
- AI topics for a described situation (needs a small backend; privacy policy changes)
- Group mode: everyone sees the same card on their own phone (realtime backend)
- Daily topic notification or home-screen widget
- iPhone version on the App Store

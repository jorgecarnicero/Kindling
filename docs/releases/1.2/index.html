Eight stops, light to deep

The real Travel stories route, as it plays in the app. Every stop has a spark: a concrete way in if nobody knows where to start.

Starts light
  1. 1

    What's the best thing you've ever eaten on a trip?

    Spark: Think of a street stall, a gas station or someone's kitchen.

  2. 2

    What's the worst place you've ever slept while traveling?

    Spark: Airports, car seats and dodgy hostels all count.

  3. 3

    Tell us about a trip where almost everything went wrong.

    Spark: Start with the moment you realized it was going badly.

  4. 4

    Who's the most memorable stranger you've met while traveling?

    Spark: What did they say or do that stuck with you?

  5. 5

    Which place was nothing like you expected?

    Spark: Better, worse, or just completely different?

  6. 6

    What's the most spontaneous decision you've ever made on a trip?

    Spark: A last-minute plan, a wrong turn you kept following, a yes you didn't see coming.

  7. 7

    Which trip changed how you see something in your everyday life?

    Spark: Food, time, money, family: pick one thing that looked different when you got back.

  8. 8

    Which trip would you relive exactly as it was, and who would you bring?

    Spark: Or: which trip should this group take next?

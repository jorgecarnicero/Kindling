Small things that keep the fire going

Everything else in 1.0, in English and Spanish.

A timer that stays out of the way

A ring that slowly drains around the card, never a ticking clock. Choose 30 seconds to 5 minutes, or turn it off and just use the cards. Tap the card to pause when the waiter arrives.

Day and night

Bright, playful stickers by day. At night the campfire is lit, with an optional crackling fire sound in the background.

Favorites

Tap the heart to save a topic, then play your favorites with another group. Or share one with a friend.

No repeats

Every card in a session is new. When you've covered them all, shuffle again or mix in another vibe.

For real moments

First dates, dinners with friends, family evenings, road trips and team events.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and it works offline.

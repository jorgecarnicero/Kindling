# Kindling 2.0: Friends around the fire

Plan agreed with Jorge on 2026-11-10. Status: planned, not started. Characters (1.3) ships first; 2.0 builds on it.

**Pitch:** bring your friends to the fire. Swap friend cards, sit together on the logs, take turns, and keep the story of your talks with each person.

**Principles:** still offline, still no accounts, still "we collect no data". Everything about friends lives on the phone. Friends are for groups sitting together; online play is a later, separate step (3.0).

## Releases

| Version | Theme | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1.3.0 | Characters | Your character, backpack, found items, first-time setup (built) |
| **2.0.0** | **Friends** | Your name, friend card (QR, link, code), add friends + Friends list, Who's here? with friends and guests on the logs, **turn mode**, simple friend page, session log, privacy policy update |
| 2.1.0 | Your story together | Full friend stats: topics together, top-category titles, time by the fire, trips together, first topic together |
| 2.2.0 | Friendship items | Cosmetics earned by playing with friends |
| 3.0.0 (someday) | Online | Group mode on everyone's phone, accounts, synced friends and stats |

**The session log starts in 2.0.0**, even though the full stats come in 2.1: when the stats page arrives, people already have their history ("14 nights with Ana") instead of starting from zero.

## 2.0.0 in detail

### 1. Your name

- A name field in "Make your character" (max 20 characters, editable later from your character screen).
- It shows on your friend card and on your log at the fire.

### 2. Friend card

- **My friend card**, from your character screen: a sticker card with your character on a log, your name and a QR code, plus **Share link** and **Copy code**.
- **What the code carries:** format version, your profile id, your name, your look, and a check digit, so typos and broken links are caught instead of being saved as garbage. About 40–50 characters, e.g. `KF1.x7Hq2…`.
- **Item numbers:** every catalog item gets a permanent number (like topic ids: never renumber or reuse). This keeps codes short and means old codes keep working when items are added. An unknown number (a code from a newer version) falls back to the default for that slot.
- **Link:** `https://jorgecarnicero.github.io/Kindling/f/#KF1.x7Hq2…`
  - The data goes after the `#`, which browsers never send to a server.
  - With Kindling installed, the link opens the app on "Add friend?" (app scheme `kindling://`).
  - Without it, the page shows the friend's name, an **Open in Kindling** button and the Google Play link.

### 3. Adding friends

- **Friends list → Add friend:** **Share link** and **Paste code** come first (easiest, and every shared link also brings new people to the app); **Scan QR** stays as an option for people sitting together.
- **Confirm:** "Add Ana to your fire?" with their character, then Add / Cancel.
- **Same person again:** friends are identified by profile id, not name. Scanning someone you already have offers "Ana changed their look, update it?".
- **Friends list:** a grid of portraits with names, sorted by last played together. Tap one for their page.
  - From the page: **Rename** (your own nickname for them) and **Remove** (deletes them and their sessions from this phone).
- **Limit:** up to 100 friends.
- **Trust:** codes aren't signed, so someone could fake items they didn't earn. Accepted among friends.

### 4. Who's here?

- **When it shows: to be decided from mockups of both options.** (A) Every game: a quick step with your last group pre-selected. (B) Only when you choose: a "Bring friends" button, otherwise you play alone. The rest of this section works for both.
- **A quick step before each game** (cards and Road trip): the logs with portraits.
  - Your last group is pre-selected, so most of the time it's one tap on **Let's go**.
  - **Just me** skips it, and the app plays exactly as today.
- **Who's in it:**
  - You, always.
  - Friends: tap a portrait to bring them to the fire.
  - **+ Guest:** a name and a random free look. Guests can be kept as "regulars" without a card.
- **Size:** 2–8 people at the fire. Two sit face to face; eight make a full circle.

### 5. Turn mode

- **Fire strip:** a strip at the top of the play and trip screens, with everyone on the logs.
  - Whoever's turn it is sits in the glow.
  - The card says "**Ana**, your turn".
- **What a turn means: to be decided from mockups of both options.** (A) "Ana, your turn": that person answers first. (B) "Ana reads": that person reads the card aloud and picks who starts, or passes it on. Whichever feels more comfortable wins.
- **Order:** clockwise, starting from a random person. Each new card (or Road trip stop) passes the turn to the next person.
- **Skip me:** passes the turn without changing the card.
- **Playing alone:** no strip.
- **Accessibility:** the turn is announced to screen readers when it changes. Every gesture keeps a button.

### 6. Simple friend page

- Their character on a log, their name, **Friends since**, **Nights at the fire** (count), **Last time** ("3 days ago").
- The rest of the stats come in 2.1, built from the same session log.

### 7. Session log

- **A "night" is one session:** from Let's go until you leave the game.
- **What each night keeps:** date, mode (cards / trip), the people (friend ids, guest names), minutes, topic ids seen, and the trip finished (if any).
- **Storage:** stats are worked out from this log, never stored as totals (the same idea as character unlocks). The newest ~1,000 nights are kept, about 200 KB at most.
- **Each phone keeps its own log.** Ana's phone and yours each count your nights together. Syncing them is what accounts would add (3.0); the profile id is already there for that move.

### 8. Privacy and Play

- **Data safety stays "no data collected":** friends, names and stats never leave the phone.
- **Privacy policy (EN/ES)** gets three additions:
  - the camera is used only to scan friend codes, and no images are saved or sent;
  - friends' names and looks are stored only on your phone, and removing a friend deletes them;
  - a friend card contains your name and look, and you choose whom to share it with.
- **Camera permission** is asked the first time someone taps Scan, never at launch.
- **Listing:** new text, video and screenshots for 2.0.

## Technical plan

- **New packages** (always `npx expo install`):
  - `expo-camera`, for scanning QR codes;
  - a QR generator drawn with react-native-svg, which we already use;
  - `expo-linking`, for opening links.
- **Pure modules, tested:**
  - `src/friends/code.ts`: encode / decode, check digit, older versions;
  - `src/friends/turns.ts`: order, next, skip;
  - `src/friends/stats.ts`: stats from sessions.
- **Stores:**
  - `kindling.friends`: friends and regular guests;
  - `kindling.sessions`: the log, capped;
  - `name` added to `kindling.profile`.
- **Web page:** `docs/f/index.html`, a static page that reads the `#` part in the browser.
- **Item numbers:** a `code` on each catalog item, with a test that they're unique and never reused.

## Later (2.1, 2.2)

**2.1 Your story together.** These stats are added to the friend page:
- **Topics talked about:** the count, plus your **top category** as a title. There's one title per category in EN/ES, e.g. "Deep talk buddies", "Story swappers", "Debate rivals".
- **Time by the fire:** total time together.
- **Trips together:** the themes you finished, as badges.
- **First topic together:** the first question you ever talked about.
- **Streak:** weeks in a row with at least one night at the fire together ("4 weeks in a row 🔥"). It's weekly because friends don't meet every day. It counts nights, not new topics, so it doesn't need a bigger topic bank. But groups who play every week will see repeats sooner, which is one more reason for the 300+ topics.
- **First, check how "noisy" the page feels:** show only a few stats at a time if it gets busy.
- **Your own page:** friends count, the friend you've played with most, total nights.

**2.2 Friendship items:**

| Item | How you earn it |
|---|---|
| Friendship bracelet (hold) | play with 1 friend |
| Matching beanies | 5 nights with the same friend |
| Campfire mug | play with 3 different friends |
| Fire crown | 20 nights with friends |

## Decisions (Jorge, 2026-11-10)

1. **Adding friends:** code + link first (easiest, and they bring new people to the app); QR stays as an option.
2. **Who's here?:** mock up both options (every game vs only when you choose), then decide.
3. **What a turn means:** mock up both ("answers first" vs "reads and picks"), then pick the more comfortable one.
4. **Stats:** keep the list, add a weekly streak, watch that the friend page doesn't get noisy.
5. **Name:** "Friends" / "Amigos".
